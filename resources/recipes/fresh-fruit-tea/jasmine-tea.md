# Jasmine Tea

Made with jasmine tea. Select the required variant before measuring ingredients.

## Equipment

- Serving Cup
- Shaker

## M — With ice

### Ingredients

| Component     | Ingredient         | Amount | Unit |
| ------------- | ------------------ | ------ | ---- |
| Drink mixture | brewed jasmine tea | 250    | ml   |
| Drink mixture | liquid sugar       | 40     | ml   |

### Method

1. **Measure brewed jasmine tea.** Add 250 ml brewed jasmine tea to shaker.
2. **Measure liquid sugar.** Add 40 ml liquid sugar to shaker.
3. **Chill the drink.** Fill the shaker with ice.
4. **Shake the mixture.** Shake the ingredients in the shaker until evenly combined.
5. **Assemble the drink.** Pour into the serving cup.

## M — No ice

### Ingredients

| Component     | Ingredient         | Amount | Unit |
| ------------- | ------------------ | ------ | ---- |
| Drink mixture | brewed jasmine tea | 250    | ml   |
| Drink mixture | liquid sugar       | 45     | ml   |
| Drink mixture | ice cubes          | 2–3    | —    |

### Method

1. **Measure brewed jasmine tea.** Add 250 ml brewed jasmine tea to shaker.
2. **Measure liquid sugar.** Add 45 ml liquid sugar to shaker.
3. **Measure ice cubes.** Add 2–3 ice cubes to the shaker for chilling.
4. **Top up the drink.** Top up with jasmine tea to the standard serving line.
5. **Shake the mixture.** Shake the ingredients in the shaker until evenly combined.
6. **Assemble the drink.** Pour into the serving cup.

### Tips

- This no-ice variant still uses 2–3 ice cubes for chilling. Keep the listed liquid and sweetener amounts; do not reuse the with-ice quantities.
