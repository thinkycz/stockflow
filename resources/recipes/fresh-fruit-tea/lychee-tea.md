# Lychee Tea

Made with lychees, jasmine tea, lychee purée, lemon syrup. Select the required variant before measuring ingredients.

## Equipment

- Serving Cup
- Shaker

## M — With ice

### Ingredients

| Component     | Ingredient         | Amount               | Unit   |
| ------------- | ------------------ | -------------------- | ------ |
| Drink mixture | lychees            | 1 regular or 2 small | —      |
| Drink mixture | lemon slices       | 1                    | pieces |
| Drink mixture | brewed jasmine tea | 250                  | ml     |
| Drink mixture | liquid sugar       | 20                   | ml     |
| Drink mixture | lychee purée       | 30                   | ml     |
| Drink mixture | lemon syrup        | 5                    | ml     |

### Method

1. **Measure lychees.** Add 1 regular or 2 small lychees to shaker.
2. **Measure lemon slices.** Add 1 lemon slice to shaker.
3. **Crush the fruit.** Crush the fruit in the shaker to release its juice.
4. **Measure brewed jasmine tea.** Add 250 ml brewed jasmine tea to shaker.
5. **Measure liquid sugar.** Add 20 ml liquid sugar to shaker.
6. **Measure lychee purée.** Add 30 ml lychee purée to shaker.
7. **Measure lemon syrup.** Add 5 ml lemon syrup to shaker.
8. **Chill the drink.** Fill the shaker with ice.
9. **Shake the mixture.** Shake the ingredients in the shaker until evenly combined.
10. **Assemble the drink.** Pour into the serving cup.

### Method

1. **Measure lychees.** Add 1 regular or 2 small lychees to shaker.
2. **Measure lemon slices.** Add 1 lemon slice to shaker.
3. **Crush the fruit.** Crush the fruit in the shaker to release its juice.
4. **Measure brewed jasmine tea.** Add 250 ml brewed jasmine tea to shaker.
5. **Measure liquid sugar.** Add 25 ml liquid sugar to shaker.
6. **Measure lychee purée.** Add 30 ml lychee purée to shaker.
