# Mango Passion Fruit Tea

Made with passion fruit pulp, jasmine tea, passion fruit syrup, mango purée. Select the required variant before measuring ingredients.

## Equipment

- Serving Cup
- Shaker
- Stirring Spoon

## M — With ice

### Ingredients

| Component     | Ingredient          | Amount           | Unit   |
| ------------- | ------------------- | ---------------- | ------ |
| Drink mixture | passion fruit pulp  | 0.5              | scoops |
| Drink mixture | mango pieces        | 0.5              | scoops |
| Drink mixture | brewed jasmine tea  | 250              | ml     |
| Drink mixture | liquid sugar        | 20               | ml     |
| Drink mixture | passion fruit syrup | 15               | ml     |
| Drink mixture | mango purée         | 15               | ml     |
| Ice           | ice cubes           | full serving cup | —      |

### Method

1. **Measure passion fruit pulp.** Add measured passion fruit pulp to shaker.
2. **Measure mango pieces.** Add measured mango pieces to shaker.
3. **Measure brewed jasmine tea.** Add measured brewed jasmine tea to shaker.
4. **Measure liquid sugar.** Add measured liquid sugar to shaker.
5. **Measure passion fruit syrup.** Add measured passion fruit syrup to shaker.
6. **Measure mango purée.** Add measured mango purée to shaker.
7. **Measure the ice.** Measure the ice according to the ingredient list, then transfer it to the shaker.
8. **Shake the mixture.** Close the shaker and shake until the ingredients are evenly combined.
9. **Assemble the drink.** Pour into the serving cup.

## M — No ice

### Ingredients

| Component     | Ingredient          | Amount          | Unit   |

| Drink mixture | liquid sugar        | 25              | ml     |
| Drink mixture | passion fruit syrup | 15              | ml     |
| Drink mixture | mango purée         | 15              | ml     |
| Ice           | ice cubes           | 2–3             | —      |
| Top-up        | brewed jasmine tea  | to serving line | —      |

### Method

1. **Measure passion fruit pulp.** Add measured passion fruit pulp to shaker.
2. **Measure mango pieces.** Add measured mango pieces to shaker.
3. **Measure brewed jasmine tea.** Add measured brewed jasmine tea to shaker.
4. **Measure liquid sugar.** Add measured liquid sugar to shaker.
5. **Measure passion fruit syrup.** Add measured passion fruit syrup to shaker.
6. **Measure mango purée.** Add measured mango purée to shaker.
7. **Measure ice cubes.** Add ice cubes to the shaker for chilling according to the ingredient list.
8. **Shake the mixture.** Close the shaker and shake until the ingredients are evenly combined.
9. **Assemble the drink.** Pour into the serving cup.
10. **Top up the drink.** Top up with brewed jasmine tea in the serving cup to the standard serving line. Stir gently to combine.

### Tips

- This no-ice variant still uses the listed ice cubes for chilling. Keep the listed liquid and sweetener amounts; do not reuse the with-ice quantities.
