# Passion Fruit Tea

Made with passion fruit pulp, jasmine tea, passion fruit syrup, passion fruit purée. Select the required variant before measuring ingredients.

## Equipment

- Serving Cup
- Shaker
- Stirring Spoon

## M — With ice

### Ingredients

| Component     | Ingredient          | Amount           | Unit   |
| ------------- | ------------------- | ---------------- | ------ |
| Drink mixture | passion fruit pulp  | 1                | scoops |
| Drink mixture | brewed jasmine tea  | 250              | ml     |
| Drink mixture | liquid sugar        | 25               | ml     |
| Drink mixture | passion fruit syrup | 15               | ml     |
| Drink mixture | passion fruit purée | 10               | ml     |
| Ice           | ice cubes           | full serving cup | —      |

### Method

1. **Measure passion fruit pulp.** Add 1 standard scoop passion fruit pulp to shaker.
2. **Measure brewed jasmine tea.** Add 250 ml brewed jasmine tea to shaker.
3. **Measure liquid sugar.** Add 25 ml liquid sugar to shaker.
4. **Measure passion fruit syrup.** Add 15 ml passion fruit syrup to shaker.
5. **Measure passion fruit purée.** Add 10 ml passion fruit purée to shaker.
6. **Add a full cup of ice.** Fill the serving cup with ice, then transfer the ice to the shaker.
7. **Shake the mixture.** Close the shaker and shake until the ingredients are evenly combined.
8. **Assemble the drink.** Pour into the serving cup.

## M — No ice

### Ingredients

| Component     | Ingredient          | Amount          | Unit   |
| ------------- | ------------------- | --------------- | ------ |
| Drink mixture | passion fruit pulp  | 1               | scoops |
| Drink mixture | brewed jasmine tea  | 250             | ml     |
| Drink mixture | liquid sugar        | 30              | ml     |
| Drink mixture | passion fruit syrup | 15              | ml     |
| Drink mixture | passion fruit purée | 10              | ml     |
| Drink mixture | ice cubes           | 2–3             | —      |
| Top-up        | brewed jasmine tea  | to serving line | —      |

### Method

1. **Measure passion fruit pulp.** Add 1 standard scoop passion fruit pulp to shaker.
2. **Measure brewed jasmine tea.** Add 250 ml brewed jasmine tea to shaker.
3. **Measure liquid sugar.** Add 30 ml liquid sugar to shaker.
4. **Measure passion fruit syrup.** Add 15 ml passion fruit syrup to shaker.
5. **Measure passion fruit purée.** Add 10 ml passion fruit purée to shaker.
6. **Measure ice cubes.** Add 2–3 ice cubes to the shaker for chilling.
7. **Shake the mixture.** Close the shaker and shake until the ingredients are evenly combined.
8. **Assemble the drink.** Pour into the serving cup.
9. **Top up the drink.** Top up with brewed jasmine tea in the serving cup to the standard serving line. Stir gently to combine.

### Tips

- This no-ice variant still uses 2–3 ice cubes for chilling. Keep the listed liquid and sweetener amounts; do not reuse the with-ice quantities.
