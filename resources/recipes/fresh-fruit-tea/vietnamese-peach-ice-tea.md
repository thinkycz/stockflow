# Vietnamese Peach Ice Tea

Made with lemongrass, Ceylon tea, peach purée, lemon syrup. Select the required variant before measuring ingredients.

## Equipment

- Serving Cup
- Shaker

## M — With ice

### Ingredients

| Component     | Ingredient        | Amount    | Unit   |
| ------------- | ----------------- | --------- | ------ |
| Drink mixture | peach slice       | 1         | pieces |
| Drink mixture | orange slice      | 1         | pieces |
| Drink mixture | lemongrass        | as needed | —      |
| Drink mixture | brewed Ceylon tea | 250       | ml     |
| Drink mixture | liquid sugar      | 20        | ml     |
| Drink mixture | peach purée       | 30        | ml     |
| Drink mixture | lemon syrup       | 5         | ml     |

### Method

1. **Measure peach slice.** Add 1 peach slice to shaker.
2. **Measure orange slice.** Add 1 orange slice to shaker.
3. **Measure lemongrass.** Add as needed lemongrass to shaker.
4. **Crush the fruit.** Crush the fruit and lemongrass in the shaker to release their juices.
5. **Measure brewed Ceylon tea.** Add 250 ml brewed Ceylon tea to shaker.
6. **Measure liquid sugar.** Add 20 ml liquid sugar to shaker.
7. **Measure peach purée.** Add 30 ml peach purée to shaker.
8. **Measure lemon syrup.** Add 5 ml lemon syrup to shaker.
9. **Chill the drink.** Fill the shaker with ice.
10. **Shake the mixture.** Shake the ingredients in the shaker until evenly combined.
11. **Assemble the drink.** Pour into the serving cup.
