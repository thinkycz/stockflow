# Bueno Matcha

Warm matcha with steamed milk, white chocolate syrup and hazelnut syrup, finished with a Nutella cloud.

## Equipment

- Serving Cup
- Milk Jug
- Steam Wand
- Matcha Bowl
- Matcha Whisk
- Mixing Bowl
- Whisk / Mixer
- Stirring Spoon

## Hot

### Ingredients

| Component       | Ingredient            | Amount | Unit |
| --------------- | --------------------- | ------ | ---- |
| Drink base      | white chocolate syrup | 5      | g    |
| Drink base      | hazelnut syrup        | 5      | g    |
| Steamed milk    | milk                  | 140    | g    |
| Matcha          | water at 70–80 °C     | 50     | g    |
| Matcha          | matcha                | 3.5    | g    |
| Cloud / topping | whipping cream        | 40     | g    |
| Cloud / topping | milk                  | 20     | g    |
| Cloud / topping | Nutella               | 15     | g    |

### Method

1. **Measure white chocolate syrup.** Add measured white chocolate syrup to serving cup.
2. **Measure hazelnut syrup.** Add measured hazelnut syrup to serving cup.
3. **Stir to combine.** Stir the ingredients until evenly combined.
4. **Measure milk.** Add measured milk to milk jug.
5. **Steam the milk.** Steam the milk in the milk jug.
6. **Measure water at 70–80 °C.** Add measured water at 70–80 °C to matcha bowl.
7. **Measure matcha.** Add measured matcha to matcha bowl.
8. **Whisk the matcha.** Whisk the powder and water until smooth, with no dry clumps remaining.
9. **Assemble the drink.** Pour the steamed milk and prepared matcha into the serving cup.
10. **Measure whipping cream.** Add measured whipping cream to mixing bowl.
11. **Measure milk.** Add measured milk to mixing bowl.
12. **Measure Nutella.** Add measured Nutella to mixing bowl.
13. **Whip the cloud.** Whip until thick.
14. **Assemble the drink.** Pour the Nutella cloud into the serving cup.
