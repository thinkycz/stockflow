# Strawberry Cloud

Warm Himawari matcha with steamed milk, a whipped strawberry cloud, and two dried strawberries.

## Equipment

- Milk Jug
- Steam Wand
- Matcha Bowl
- Matcha Whisk
- Mixing Bowl
- Whisk / Mixer
- Serving Cup

## Hot

### Ingredients

| Component       | Ingredient         | Amount | Unit   |
| --------------- | ------------------ | ------ | ------ |
| Steamed milk    | milk               | 140    | g      |
| Matcha          | water at 70–80 °C  | 50     | g      |
| Matcha          | Himawari matcha    | 3.5    | g      |
| Cloud / topping | whipping cream     | 30     | g      |
| Cloud / topping | milk               | 30     | g      |
| Cloud / topping | strawberry syrup   | 30     | g      |
| Finish          | dried strawberries | 2      | pieces |

### Method

1. **Measure milk.** Add 140 g milk to milk jug.
2. **Steam the milk.** Steam the milk in the milk jug.
3. **Measure water at 70–80 °C.** Add 50 g water at 70–80 °C to matcha bowl.
4. **Measure Himawari matcha.** Add 3.5 g Himawari matcha to matcha bowl.
5. **Whisk the matcha.** Whisk until smooth and no dry clumps remain.
6. **Assemble the drink.** Pour the steamed milk and prepared matcha into the serving cup.
7. **Measure whipping cream.** Add 30 g whipping cream to mixing bowl.
8. **Measure milk.** Add 30 g milk to mixing bowl.
9. **Measure strawberry syrup.** Add 30 g strawberry syrup to mixing bowl.
10. **Whip the cloud.** Whip the cloud mixture until thick.
11. **Assemble the drink.** Spoon the strawberry cloud over the drink.
12. **Finish the drink.** Finish with 2 dried strawberries.
