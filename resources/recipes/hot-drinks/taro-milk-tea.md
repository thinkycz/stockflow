# Taro Milk Tea

A smooth taro drink made by dissolving milk powder and taro powder in hot water before adding milk.

## Equipment

- Serving Cup
- Mixing Vessel

## Hot

### Ingredients

| Component    | Ingredient       | Amount | Unit   |
| ------------ | ---------------- | ------ | ------ |
| Taro mixture | hot water        | 200    | g      |
| Taro mixture | milk powder      | 3      | scoops |
| Taro mixture | taro powder      | 2      | scoops |
| Taro mixture | granulated sugar | 5      | g      |
| Taro mixture | milk             | 100    | g      |

### Method

1. **Measure hot water.** Add measured hot water to mixing vessel.
2. **Measure milk powder.** Add measured milk powder to mixing vessel.
3. **Measure taro powder.** Add measured taro powder to mixing vessel.
4. **Measure granulated sugar.** Add measured granulated sugar to mixing vessel.
5. **Mix until smooth.** Mix the hot water, powders, and sugar until dissolved and smooth.
6. **Measure milk.** Add measured milk to mixing vessel.
7. **Mix until smooth.** Mix again until the milk is fully combined.
8. **Assemble the drink.** Pour the prepared taro milk tea into the serving cup.
