# Milky Matcha Cloud

Made with milk, sweetened condensed milk (Salko), whipping cream, matcha. Select the required variant before measuring ingredients.

## Equipment

- Serving Cup
- Mixing Bowl
- Whisk / Mixer
- Stirring Spoon

## S — With ice

### Ingredients

| Component       | Ingredient                       | Amount           | Unit |
| --------------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------- | ---- |
| Drink base      | milk                             | 100              | ml   |
| Drink base      | sweetened condensed milk (Salko) | 20               | ml   |
| Ice             | ice cubes                        | full serving cup | —    |
| Cloud / topping | whipping cream                   | 40               | ml   |
| Cloud / topping | milk                             | 40               | ml   |
| Cloud / topping | matcha                           | 3.5              | g    |
| Finish          | matcha powder                    | as needed        | —    |

### Method

1. **Measure milk.** Add 100 ml milk to serving cup.
2. **Measure sweetened condensed milk (Salko).** Add 20 ml sweetened condensed milk (Salko) to serving cup.
3. **Stir to combine.** Stir the ingredients until evenly combined.
4. **Fill the cup with ice.** Fill the serving cup with ice.
5. **Measure whipping cream.** Add 40 ml whipping cream to mixing bowl.
6. **Measure milk.** Add 40 ml milk to mixing bowl.
7. **Measure matcha.** Add 3.5 g matcha to mixing bowl.
8. **Whip the cloud.** Whip until thick.
9. **Assemble the drink.** Pour the matcha cloud into the serving cup.
10. **Finish the drink.** Garnish with matcha powder as needed.

## S — No ice

### Ingredients

| Component       | Ingredient                       | Amount          | Unit |
| --------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------- | ---- |
| Drink base      | milk                             | 100             | ml   |
| Drink base      | sweetened condensed milk (Salko) | 20              | ml   |
| Drink base      | liquid sugar                     | 5               | ml   |
| Ice             | ice cubes                        | 2–3             | —    |
| Top-up          | milk                             | to serving line | —    |
| Cloud / topping | whipping cream                   | 40              | ml   |
| Cloud / topping | milk                             | 40              | ml   |
| Cloud / topping | matcha                           | 3.5             | g    |
| Finish          | matcha powder                    | as needed       | —    |

### Method

1. **Measure milk.** Add 100 ml milk to serving cup.
2. **Measure sweetened condensed milk (Salko).** Add 20 ml sweetened condensed milk (Salko) to serving cup.
3. **Stir to combine.** Stir the ingredients until evenly combined.
4. **Measure ice cubes.** Add 2–3 ice cubes to the serving cup for chilling.
5. **Measure liquid sugar.** Add 5 ml liquid sugar to serving cup.
6. **Measure whipping cream.** Add 40 ml whipping cream to mixing bowl.
7. **Measure milk.** Add 40 ml milk to mixing bowl.
8. **Measure matcha.** Add 3.5 g matcha to mixing bowl.
9. **Whip the cloud.** Whip until thick.
10. **Top up the drink.** Top up with milk in the serving cup to the standard serving line, leaving room for the cloud. Stir the base gently to combine.
11. **Assemble the drink.** Pour the matcha cloud into the serving cup.
12. **Finish the drink.** Garnish with matcha powder as needed.

### Tips

- This no-ice variant still uses 2–3 ice cubes for chilling. Keep the listed liquid and sweetener amounts; do not reuse the with-ice quantities.
