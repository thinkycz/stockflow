# Bueno Hojicha

Oat milk hojicha with white chocolate syrup and hazelnut syrup, finished with a Nutella cloud.

## Equipment

- Serving Cup
- Matcha Bowl
- Matcha Whisk
- Mixing Bowl
- Whisk / Mixer
- Stirring Spoon

## With ice

### Ingredients

| Component       | Ingredient            | Amount           | Unit |
| --------------- | --------------------- | ---------------- | ---- |
| Drink base      | white chocolate syrup | 10               | g    |
| Drink base      | hazelnut syrup        | 10               | g    |
| Drink base      | oat milk              | 140              | g    |
| Hojicha         | water at 70–80 °C     | 60               | g    |
| Hojicha         | hojicha powder        | 4.5              | g    |
| Ice             | ice cubes             | full serving cup | —    |
| Cloud / topping | whipping cream        | 40               | g    |
| Cloud / topping | milk                  | 20               | g    |
| Cloud / topping | Nutella               | 15               | g    |

### Method

1. **Measure white chocolate syrup.** Add measured white chocolate syrup to serving cup.
2. **Measure hazelnut syrup.** Add measured hazelnut syrup to serving cup.
3. **Measure oat milk.** Add measured oat milk to serving cup.
4. **Stir to combine.** Stir the ingredients until evenly combined.
5. **Add ice.** Add ice to the serving cup according to the ingredient list.
6. **Measure water at 70–80 °C.** Add measured water at 70–80 °C to matcha bowl.
7. **Measure hojicha powder.** Add measured hojicha powder to matcha bowl.
8. **Whisk the hojicha.** Whisk the powder and water until smooth, with no dry clumps remaining.
9. **Assemble the drink.** Pour the prepared hojicha into the serving cup.
10. **Measure whipping cream.** Add measured whipping cream to mixing bowl.
11. **Measure milk.** Add measured milk to mixing bowl.
12. **Measure Nutella.** Add measured Nutella to mixing bowl.
13. **Whip the cloud.** Whip until thick.
14. **Assemble the drink.** Pour the Nutella cloud into the serving cup.
