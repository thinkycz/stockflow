# Bueno Matcha

Oat milk matcha with white chocolate syrup and hazelnut syrup, finished with a Nutella cloud.

## Equipment

- Serving Cup
- Matcha Bowl
- Matcha Whisk
- Mixing Bowl
- Whisk / Mixer
- Stirring Spoon

## Standard

### Ingredients

| Component       | Ingredient            | Amount | Unit |
| --------------- | --------------------- | ------ | ---- |
| Drink base      | white chocolate syrup | 10     | g    |
| Drink base      | hazelnut syrup        | 10     | g    |
| Drink base      | oat milk              | 140    | g    |
| Matcha          | water at 70–80 °C     | 60     | g    |
| Matcha          | matcha                | 4.5    | g    |
| Cloud / topping | whipping cream        | 40     | g    |
| Cloud / topping | milk                  | 20     | g    |
| Cloud / topping | Nutella               | 15     | g    |

### Method

1. **Measure white chocolate syrup.** Add 10 g white chocolate syrup to serving cup.
2. **Measure hazelnut syrup.** Add 10 g hazelnut syrup to serving cup.
3. **Measure oat milk.** Add 140 g oat milk to serving cup.
4. **Stir to combine.** Stir the ingredients until evenly combined.
5. **Measure water at 70–80 °C.** Add 60 g water at 70–80 °C to matcha bowl.
6. **Measure matcha.** Add 4.5 g matcha to matcha bowl.
7. **Whisk the matcha.** Whisk the powder and water until smooth, with no dry clumps remaining.
8. **Assemble the drink.** Pour the matcha into the serving cup.
9. **Measure whipping cream.** Add 40 g whipping cream to mixing bowl.
10. **Measure milk.** Add 20 g milk to mixing bowl.
11. **Measure Nutella.** Add 15 g Nutella to mixing bowl.
12. **Whip the cloud.** Whip until thick.
13. **Assemble the drink.** Pour the Nutella cloud into the serving cup.
