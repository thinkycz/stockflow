# Pumpkin Spice Matcha

Oat milk matcha with pumpkin spice syrup, a gingerbread cloud and a Lotus biscuit topping.

## Equipment

- Serving Cup
- Matcha Bowl
- Matcha Whisk
- Mixing Bowl
- Whisk / Mixer
- Stirring Spoon

## Standard

### Ingredients

| Component       | Ingredient           | Amount    | Unit |
| --------------- | -------------------- | --------- | ---- |
| Drink base      | pumpkin spice syrup  | 10        | g    |
| Drink base      | oat milk             | 140       | g    |
| Matcha          | water at 70–80 °C    | 60        | g    |
| Matcha          | matcha               | 4.5       | g    |
| Cloud / topping | whipping cream       | 40        | g    |
| Cloud / topping | milk                 | 40        | g    |
| Cloud / topping | gingerbread syrup    | 10        | g    |
| Finish          | Lotus biscuit crumbs | as needed | —    |

### Method

1. **Measure pumpkin spice syrup.** Add 10 g pumpkin spice syrup to serving cup.
2. **Measure oat milk.** Add 140 g oat milk to serving cup.
3. **Stir to combine.** Stir the ingredients until evenly combined.
4. **Measure water at 70–80 °C.** Add 60 g water at 70–80 °C to matcha bowl.
5. **Measure matcha.** Add 4.5 g matcha to matcha bowl.
6. **Whisk the matcha.** Whisk the powder and water until smooth, with no dry clumps remaining.
7. **Assemble the drink.** Pour the matcha into the serving cup.
8. **Measure whipping cream.** Add 40 g whipping cream to mixing bowl.
9. **Measure milk.** Add 40 g milk to mixing bowl.
10. **Measure gingerbread syrup.** Add 10 g gingerbread syrup to mixing bowl.
11. **Whip the cloud.** Whip until thick.
12. **Assemble the drink.** Pour the gingerbread cloud into the serving cup.
13. **Finish the drink.** Garnish with Lotus biscuit crumbs as needed.
