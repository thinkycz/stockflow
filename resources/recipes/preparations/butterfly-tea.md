# Butterfly Tea

Prepare the butterfly tea batch before making drinks. Follow the selected batch size and the written timing carefully.

## Equipment

- Container
- Fine Strainer

## Standard

### Ingredients

| Component | Ingredient            | Amount                | Unit |
| --------- | --------------------- | --------------------- | ---- |
| Batch     | dry butterfly pea tea | 5                     | g    |
| Batch     | hot water             | 300                   | ml   |
| Ice       | ice cubes             | to 600 ml total batch | —    |

### Method

1. **Measure dry butterfly pea tea.** Add 5 g dry butterfly pea tea to container.
2. **Measure hot water.** Add 300 ml hot water to container.
3. **Steep the tea.** Steep until the tea is a deep, dark blue, then strain out the tea leaves.
4. **Chill the batch.** Add ice until the total volume reaches 600 ml.
