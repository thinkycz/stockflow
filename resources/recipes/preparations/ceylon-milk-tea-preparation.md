# Ceylon Milk Tea Preparation

Prepare the ceylon milk tea preparation batch before making drinks. Follow the selected batch size and the written timing carefully.

## Equipment

- Two Tea Bags
- Pot
- Pot Lid
- Stirring Spoon

## 3.5 L batch

### Ingredients

| Component | Ingredient        | Amount               | Unit |
| --------- | ----------------- | -------------------- | ---- |
| Tea blend | Ceylon tea leaves | 60                   | g    |
| Tea blend | Yunnan tea        | 30                   | g    |
| Tea blend | oolong tea leaves | 10                   | g    |
| Batch     | hot water         | 2.5                  | L    |
| Batch     | milk powder       | 900                  | g    |
| Ice       | ice cubes         | to 3.5 L total batch | —    |

### Method

1. **Measure Ceylon tea leaves.** Divide measured Ceylon tea leaves between the tea bags.
2. **Measure Yunnan tea.** Divide measured Yunnan tea between the same tea bags.
3. **Measure oolong tea leaves.** Divide measured oolong tea leaves between the same tea bags.
4. **Measure hot water.** Add measured hot water to pot. Immerse the filled tea bags in the water.
5. **Cook the batch.** Cook the covered tea for 10 minutes. Remove the tea bags before continuing.
6. **Measure milk powder.** Add measured milk powder to pot.
7. **Stir to combine.** Stir until completely dissolved.
8. **Chill the batch.** Add ice until the total batch volume matches the ingredient list.
