# Ceylon Milk Tea Preparation

Prepare the ceylon milk tea preparation batch before making drinks. Follow the selected batch size and the written timing carefully.

## Equipment

- Two Tea Bags
- Pot

## 3.5 L batch

### Ingredients

| Component | Ingredient        | Amount | Unit |
| --------- | ----------------- | ------ | ---- |
| Tea blend | Ceylon tea leaves | 60     | g    |
| Tea blend | Yunnan tea        | 30     | g    |
| Tea blend | oolong tea leaves | 10     | g    |
| Batch     | hot water         | 2.5    | L    |
| Batch     | milk powder       | 900    | g    |

### Method

1. **Measure Ceylon tea leaves.** Add 60 g Ceylon tea leaves to two tea bags.
2. **Measure Yunnan tea.** Add 30 g Yunnan tea to two tea bags.
3. **Measure oolong tea leaves.** Add 10 g oolong tea leaves to two tea bags.
4. **Measure hot water.** Add 2.5 L hot water to pot.
5. **Cook the batch.** Cook the covered tea for 10 minutes.
6. **Measure milk powder.** Add 900 g milk powder to pot.
7. **Stir to combine.** Stir until completely dissolved.
8. **Chill the batch.** Add ice until the total volume reaches 3.5 L.
