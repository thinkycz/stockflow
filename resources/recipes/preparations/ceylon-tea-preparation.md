# Ceylon Tea Preparation

Prepare the ceylon tea preparation batch before making drinks. Follow the selected batch size and the written timing carefully.

## Equipment

- Pot
- Pot Lid
- Fine Strainer

## Standard

### Ingredients

| Component | Ingredient        | Amount               | Unit |
| --------- | ----------------- | -------------------- | ---- |
| Batch     | Ceylon tea leaves | 60                   | g    |
| Batch     | water             | 2.5                  | L    |
| Ice       | ice cubes         | to 3.5 L total batch | —    |

### Method

1. **Measure Ceylon tea leaves.** Add 60 g Ceylon tea leaves to pot.
2. **Measure water.** Add 2.5 L water to pot.
3. **Heat the mixture.** Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer for 10 minutes. Strain out the tea leaves before adding ice.
4. **Chill the batch.** Add ice until the total volume reaches 3.5 L.
