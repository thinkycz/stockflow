# Jasmine Tea Preparation

Prepare the jasmine tea preparation batch before making drinks. Follow the selected batch size and the written timing carefully.

## Equipment

- Tea Bag
- Container

## Standard

### Ingredients

| Component | Ingredient         | Amount             | Unit |
| --------- | ------------------ | ------------------ | ---- |
| Tea blend | jasmine tea leaves | 35                 | g    |
| Batch     | water at 90 °C     | 2.5                | L    |
| Ice       | ice cubes          | to 4 L total batch | —    |

### Method

1. **Measure jasmine tea leaves.** Add 35 g jasmine tea leaves to tea bag.
2. **Measure water at 90 °C.** Add 2.5 L water at 90 °C to container. Immerse the filled tea bag in the water.
3. **Steep the tea.** Steep for 10 minutes. Remove the tea bag before continuing.
4. **Chill the batch.** Add ice until the total volume reaches 4 L.
