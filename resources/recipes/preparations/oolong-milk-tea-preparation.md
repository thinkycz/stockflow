# Oolong Milk Tea Preparation

Prepare the oolong milk tea preparation batch before making drinks. Follow the selected batch size and the written timing carefully.

## Equipment

- Two Tea Bags
- Container
- Tea Bag

## 3.5 L batch

### Ingredients

| Component | Ingredient        | Amount | Unit |
| --------- | ----------------- | ------ | ---- |
| Tea blend | oolong tea leaves | 70     | g    |
| Tea blend | Ceylon tea leaves | 30     | g    |
| Batch     | water at 90 °C    | 2.5    | L    |
| Batch     | milk powder       | 900    | g    |

### Method

1. **Measure oolong tea leaves.** Add 70 g oolong tea leaves to two tea bags.
2. **Measure Ceylon tea leaves.** Add 30 g Ceylon tea leaves to two tea bags.
3. **Measure water at 90 °C.** Add 2.5 L water at 90 °C to container.
4. **Steep the tea.** Steep for 10 minutes.
5. **Measure milk powder.** Add 900 g milk powder to container.
6. **Stir to combine.** Stir until completely dissolved.
7. **Chill the batch.** Add ice until the total volume reaches 3.5 L.

## 1.5 L batch

### Ingredients

| Component | Ingredient        | Amount | Unit |
| --------- | ----------------- | ------ | ---- |
| Tea blend | oolong tea leaves | 20     | g    |
| Tea blend | Ceylon tea leaves | 10     | g    |
| Batch     | water at 90 °C    | 1      | L    |
| Batch     | milk powder       | 300    | g    |

### Method

1. **Measure oolong tea leaves.** Add 20 g oolong tea leaves to tea bag.
2. **Measure Ceylon tea leaves.** Add 10 g Ceylon tea leaves to tea bag.
3. **Measure water at 90 °C.** Add 1 L water at 90 °C to container.
4. **Steep the tea.** Steep for 10 minutes.
5. **Measure milk powder.** Add 300 g milk powder to container.
6. **Stir to combine.** Stir until completely dissolved.
7. **Chill the batch.** Add ice until the total volume reaches 1.5 L.
