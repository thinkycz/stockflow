# Double Strawberry

Made with strawberry purée, milk, matcha, whipping cream. Select the required variant before measuring ingredients.

## Equipment

- Serving Cup
- Matcha Bowl
- Matcha Whisk
- Mixing Bowl
- Whisk / Mixer

## M — With ice

### Ingredients

| Component       | Ingredient         | Amount    | Unit |
| --------------- | ------------------ | --------- | ---- |
| Drink base      | strawberry purée   | 30        | ml   |
| Drink base      | milk               | 140       | ml   |
| Matcha          | water at 70–80 °C  | 50        | ml   |
| Matcha          | matcha             | 4.5       | g    |
| Cloud / topping | whipping cream     | 40        | ml   |
| Cloud / topping | milk               | 40        | ml   |
| Cloud / topping | strawberry syrup   | 40        | ml   |
| Finish          | dried strawberries | as needed | —    |

### Method

1. **Measure strawberry purée.** Add 30 ml strawberry purée to serving cup.
2. **Chill the drink.** Fill the serving cup with ice.
3. **Measure milk.** Add 140 ml milk to serving cup.
4. **Measure water at 70–80 °C.** Add 50 ml water at 70–80 °C to matcha bowl.
5. **Measure matcha.** Add 4.5 g matcha to matcha bowl.
6. **Whisk the matcha.** Whisk the powder and water until smooth, with no dry clumps remaining.
7. **Assemble the drink.** Pour the matcha into the serving cup.
8. **Measure whipping cream.** Add 40 ml whipping cream to mixing bowl.
9. **Measure milk.** Add 40 ml milk to mixing bowl.
10. **Measure strawberry syrup.** Add 40 ml strawberry syrup to mixing bowl.
11. **Whip the cloud.** Whip until thick.
12. **Assemble the drink.** Pour the strawberry cloud into the serving cup.
13. **Finish the drink.** Garnish with as needed dried strawberries.

## M — No ice

### Ingredients

| Component       | Ingredient         | Amount    | Unit |
| --------------- | ------------------ | --------- | ---- |
| Drink base      | strawberry purée   | 30        | ml   |
| Drink base      | ice cubes          | 2–3       | —    |
| Drink base      | milk               | 140       | ml   |
| Drink base      | liquid sugar       | 5         | ml   |
| Matcha          | water at 70–80 °C  | 50        | ml   |
| Matcha          | matcha             | 4.5       | g    |
| Cloud / topping | whipping cream     | 40        | ml   |
| Cloud / topping | milk               | 40        | ml   |
| Cloud / topping | strawberry syrup   | 40        | ml   |
| Finish          | dried strawberries | as needed | —    |

### Method

1. **Measure strawberry purée.** Add 30 ml strawberry purée to serving cup.
2. **Measure ice cubes.** Add 2–3 ice cubes to the serving cup for chilling.
3. **Measure milk.** Add 140 ml milk to serving cup.
4. **Measure liquid sugar.** Add 5 ml liquid sugar to serving cup.
5. **Stir to combine.** Stir the ingredients until evenly combined.
6. **Top up the drink.** Top up with milk to the standard serving line.
7. **Measure water at 70–80 °C.** Add 50 ml water at 70–80 °C to matcha bowl.
8. **Measure matcha.** Add 4.5 g matcha to matcha bowl.
9. **Whisk the matcha.** Whisk the powder and water until smooth, with no dry clumps remaining.
10. **Assemble the drink.** Pour the matcha into the serving cup.
11. **Measure whipping cream.** Add 40 ml whipping cream to mixing bowl.
12. **Measure milk.** Add 40 ml milk to mixing bowl.
13. **Measure strawberry syrup.** Add 40 ml strawberry syrup to mixing bowl.
14. **Whip the cloud.** Whip until thick.
15. **Assemble the drink.** Pour the strawberry cloud into the serving cup.
16. **Finish the drink.** Garnish with as needed dried strawberries.

### Tips

- This no-ice variant still uses 2–3 ice cubes for chilling. Keep the listed liquid and sweetener amounts; do not reuse the with-ice quantities.

## Notes

Available in size M only.
